    Home network

    While other devices are allowed, anything on your home network can play and stop torrents without a password. 8090 is the port TorrServer apps expect.

    Home network settings can only be changed on the computer that runs TorrStream Lite.

    More cache keeps more of the video around where you're watching: smoother playback on slow torrents and quicker seeks back, for more memory. It applies to the next torrent you add.
